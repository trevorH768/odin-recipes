Chicken Noodle Soup

Description

A hearty bowl of slow cooked tender chicken with egg noodles, celery,carrots and peas.

Ingredients

  • 1lbs of chicken breast
  • 750ml chicken stock
  • 2 medium carrots
  • 2-3 stalks of celery
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic
  • 2 medium bay leaves
  • 2 teaspoons Thyme
  • Salt and Pepper to taste
  • 2 table spoons olive oil
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 cup egg noodles

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients
  2. Put a medium stock pot on medium high heat with 2 tablespoons of olive olive to preheat while you are chopping
  3. Chop carrots, celery and garlic into bite sized chunks and set aside
  4. Slice chicken into bite sized pieces and place into pre heated pot with garlic
  5. Once chicken is brown, remove from the heat and set to the side
  6. Allow pot to heat back up a little and then add carrots and celery to the pot and sweat for 5 mins
  7. Once carrots and celery become slightly soft, add chicken back into the pot and stir for a few minutes to marry the flavors
  8. Now add in chicken stock and give the pot a good stir
  9. After contents of the pot start to simmer, add in previously heated carrots and celery along with Bay leaves and Thyme.
  10. Add noodles and 1 cup of water
  11. Allow to simmer for at least an hour or all day in your favorite slow cooker!
